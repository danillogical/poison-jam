A2: deliver the GPU interrupt line, and the frame producer runs

The title's frame event at guest 0x0019D630 is signalled by the producer
0x00193D90, which is only reachable through the DPC routine 0x00194480, which
is only queued by the guest ISR 0x00193C50, which is only entered from the
card's interrupt line. The line had no source.

Root cause, measured two independent ways:

- Static: `pcrtc_write(NV_PCRTC_INTR_0)` and `pmc_write(NV_PMC_INTR_0)` are
  write-1-to-clear, and the kernel's synthetic vblank asserted them by OR-ing
  into the aperture, which clears instead of sets.
- Runtime: with that path enabled the ISR ran and queued a DPC 968 times, the
  published model snapshot read NV_PMC_INTR_0 = 0 and NV_PCRTC_INTR_0 = 0, and
  0x00193D90 appeared zero times in the log.

The toolkit now gives the model a display clock (`nv2a_vblank_pulse`) and a
real interrupt line (`nv2a_set_irq_sink`, replacing two no-op `pci_irq_*`
stubs). The guest's own write-1-to-clear remains the only acknowledgment, and
both of the guest's enables gate delivery. `RECOMP_VBLANK` no longer exists:
the source is part of the model, not an override.

Measured, strict profile (no override that answers a poll), 30 s:

- `[RECOVERED] 0x00193D90 returned; ABI verified` -- the producer ran and its
  spin on NV_PMC_INTR_0 bit 24 exited, which can only happen if the guest's
  own W1C cleared the model's pending bit.
- The waiters on 0x0019D630 went from 3 to 2: one intended waiter left the
  wait and made progress.
- The run then died 3.5 s in on a newly reachable defect, not on this one:
  `[RECOVERED] ABI FAILURE 0x00048190 esp 00F7FDA0->00F7FD80 expected +8`, a
  COM vtable method whose recovered body consumes 0x20 bytes it should not.

Also in this commit: `xbox_Nv2aAttachIrqLine`, the display clock in the
standalone service thread, `NV_PCRTC_INTR_EN_0` in the diagnostic snapshot,
and a deterministic interrupt-controller fixture in `jsrf_nv2a_registers`
(source assertion, block mask, master mask, guest W1C acknowledgment, no
spurious repeat while unacknowledged).
