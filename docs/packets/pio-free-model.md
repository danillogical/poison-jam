### Limits and stops
- **Horizon:** this row is entirely decidable offline, with existing-prefix corroboration only: **neither runtime horizon bounds this discovery** and it never requires a later guest checkpoint. At the accepted same-exe/XBE strict r8 build, trapped `RECOMP_APU_TRAP=1` ended in the A2h OOM at **4.77 s** after its GP clear, while the default untrapped run lasted **31.71 s** but was **hung in the pending-word spin** (`F=2`, `Wf0=3` from its own mapping-valid dump); duration is not progress (`docs/reviews/pio-free-strict-horizon.md:32-39,73-107`). Neither duration is guaranteed and the archival correlation does not prove the OOM mechanism. A subsequent claim needing trapped guest time beyond its actual pre-OOM record names **`A2h` as prerequisite**; an untrapped run is **not** a longer-budget substitute and any future claim from it must establish a valid modelled cause **and explain why the guest is not merely hung**. No strict spin-exit/progress claim is attempted here.
- **Does not establish:** that `0x80` is true hardware state, that the 28 gate-only reads actually exit, guest observation of the GP's zero, exit from `loc_001A18D0`, boot/liveness, DirectSound/audio/VP output, GP/EP correctness, hardware timing, register-indirect/computed/table-driven access beyond A4p E3, or a complete x86 convention proof. Carry A4p's **inferred C1–C4 calling-convention premise**, possible whole-program custom `edx` pass-through, static-only direct-read scope, and lack of true-value/timing evidence. Neither this discovery nor its archive satisfies a strict criterion or claims anything works. The accepted two-leg result and `A4b2-r8` remain closed; `A4b2-r7` remains `R2-EXPL-INPUT`; no `A4b1-r4` edits.
- **Stop if:** original assets/saves or unrelated work would change; a build, guest run, instrument, or toolkit change is proposed as part of this *offline* experiment; source/identity/provenance or coverage cannot be reconciled. Missing/partial material selects O-IDENTITY or O-UNKNOWN as specified, never O-SPEC. **Write scope:** Session evidence record only; no toolkit/game code, generated files, policy or prior packets. No instrumentation exists; nothing is enabled at closure. Toolkit advance later requires new P4 bridge before any inherited P4 claim.
