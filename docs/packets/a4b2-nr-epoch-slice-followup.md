Check the reset vector plus interrupt `0x02/0x04/0x08` and all cross-image entries that could write/read slice state, including returns and DMA writes; unknown indirect targets or wrong-time words stay UNKNOWN. If chain lands on second-image values, identify their **actual DMA source bytes/provenance** independently of destination watch (including guest-staged firmware → guest-written); unresolvable source → UNKNOWN leaf. 2. **Mandatory interrupt scope, including faults.** Revalidate `docs/reviews/a4b2-nr-epoch-slice-interrupt-preparation.md:19-86` against pinned toolkit: actual SR/mask and enabled state, exhaustive GP interrupt table and raisers (Reset `0x00`, Stack Error `0x02`, Illegal `0x04`, Trap `0x08`), vector target word versions, possible triggers before exchange, handler locations/returns and their writes to any slice register/X/P/DMA/guard state. 3. **Backward closure, not forward enumeration.** Seed separate worklists at **each of the five field definitions and each write/trigger guard** that can determine the named first `B+0x810` event; bind descriptor-to-trigger consumption, DMA source/length/flags and guest VA (including earlier/reordered candidate writes). For each demanded register, flag, X/Y/P word, memory/DMA transfer, branch condition and caller context, enumerate **only its may-reaching definitions** over version-correct bytes and feasible entry/return/alias edges; recurse until a leaf. Keep PC/epoch/word version, use/def, alias set, edge feasibility and source on a finite visited `(version,PC,location,context)` worklist (cycles solved conservatively to fixed point or precise UNKNOWN). Classify **every** frontier leaf: immediate, guest-written or modeled (with source/coverage proof) → CLOSED; stub-derived MIXBUF or `0xFFFFB3` with a **concrete feasible causal chain to field or guard** → REFUTED; unresolved feasibility/alias/byte/provenance/unmodeled source → UNKNOWN with the precise node, address, edge and missing witness. 4. **Load-bearing descriptor-disjointness proof.** On image-I correct bytes re-enumerate every caller of `P 00DB` (leads `0007,007E,0090`) and sibling `P 00EB` (`000E,0019`), their context-sensitive `r0` reaching definitions, and the five actual store effective addresses at each call.
