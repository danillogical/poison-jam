# Planner ruling — A2h repeat frozen-row interpretation

**Ruling:** `O-READ-PATH` → `A2h-terminal-read-path-audit`, narrowly construed as an **audit referral**, not a causal finding. This rules on the frozen `A2h-arming-coverage-repeat-r1` contract, not on a new experiment. The repeat inherits the r2 rows from `docs/packets/a2h-slot-within-run-attribution.md:21-31` through `docs/packets/a2h-arming-coverage-attribution.md:17` and `docs/packets/a2h-arming-coverage-repeat.md:4,10-13`. The contemporaneous evidence is `docs/reviews/a2h-arming-coverage-repeat-execution-evidence.md:39-101`; the comparator's expressly limited record is `docs/reviews/a2h-run2-target-run-local-record.md:24-54`.

**Intended reading of “complete continuous no-zero-write coverage”: coverage claim.** I meant complete, continuous and positively reconciled coverage of the specified canonical DR and protected alias observation surfaces, **within which no zero-write was observed**. I did **not** mean an independently proved universal proposition that no write occurred. The inherited predecessor explicitly conditions the read-path row on positively established all-thread arming, trapped install, protected mapped aliases, stable terminal mapping and coherent records (`docs/packets/a2h-slot-read-path-displacement.md:42-51`). Absence of a DR hit alone, or even repeated absent hits, never establishes absence of a write or a writer's identity.

**Application:** The three qualifying TARGETs (pinned r2 run 2 and new runs 2 and 5) independently match the terminal triple and site; each has the positive trapped-install control `80000115 → FE000104`, continuous 17/17 reconciled arms with zero overflow, 28/28 protected mapped aliases with zero touches and no publication loss, and re-proved terminal mapping. Generated target and independent hook read both return zero. DR/mapping records reconcile without a hit; no post-hit value is claimed when there is no hit. The positive coverage, control, mapping and two observed terminal values are **not shared silence**; the absence of hit/touch is interpreted only inside those positively bounded observation surfaces. No unique positive guest/host/transient writer witness supports those rows. The third leg is a coverage/mapping reconciliation here, **not a third independent zero read**. The repeat packet's “all three legs independently certify” gate means each run must independently establish the generated target, hook read, and DR/mapping reconciliation; it does **not** mean a post-hit value must exist in a run with no DR hit. A post-hit value is conditional on a hit: demanding an additional terminal-gap DR hit would make the no-zero-write-observed audit row unavailable precisely when its observation is clean. The positive install trap tests that the DR watch actually fires, while positive per-thread arms, alias protections and terminal mapping/record reconciliation certify the observation interval; the zero-hit result alone certifies nothing. On the stipulated evidence and its complete per-run certification, the inherited `O-READ-PATH` audit predicate, rather than the missing-edge `O-COVERAGE` predicate, is selected. K=3 permits agreement on this **limited row** and reproducibility of the measured terminal/control/census facts; it does not convert unknown provenance into a negative-write or causal finding.

**Scope and limits:** The terminal slot value and generated target are both observed zero, with the specified coverage and mapping checks passing in these runs. The zero is **consistent with a still-unexplained read-path or mapping/observation discrepancy**, which the named successor must **audit**; because the two reads agree, this row does not demonstrate that they disagree, a miscompile, or any particular discrepancy. **Writer `UNKNOWN`; mechanism `UNKNOWN`.** It does not assert that the slot was never written, that no zero-write occurred outside what this instrument could observe, or that a guest, host or other named writer wrote it. In particular it must not turn “no observed hit” into “nothing happened,” even with K≥2. It is not a repair or strict functional acceptance.

**Tension and wording:** The frozen wording is genuinely ambiguous: “no-zero-write coverage” and the predecessor's “no terminal-gap zero-write” can be read as a categorical no-write assertion, while its positive-coverage conditions and explicit no-silence prohibition require the narrower observational reading. I intended the latter. **Wording fix needed for future readers, not a retroactive rewrite of the frozen packet:** say “complete continuous, positively certified coverage of specified canonical/alias writer-observation surfaces, with no zero-write *observed on those surfaces*; stable mapping; two observed terminal-zero reads; DR/mapping reconciliation. Audit referral only; writer and actual write history remain UNKNOWN.” Also spell out that a zero-hit third leg is reconciliation of coverage and mapping, not an independent post-hit value, and that K agreement may rely on repeated positive controls/reads, never repeated silence alone.

**General lesson:** A positive demonstration that observation channels were continuously available makes “no event observed on those channels” meaningful *within their scope*; it does not establish the unrestricted claim “no event happened.” Keep measured value, instrument coverage, event absence, writer attribution and causal explanation as separate propositions. If any asserted positive coverage, mapping, read or record-reconciliation leg fails on independent review, use `O-COVERAGE` and name that smallest missing edge instead; do not rescue the row by the other runs' silence. No additional run is authorized by this ruling.
